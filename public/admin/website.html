No cookies and nothing stored in the visitor's browser. A visitor is a hash of their IP address and browser with a random salt that is deleted at the end of each UTC day: one person is one visitor per day, and cannot be followed from one day to the next. Arrived counts visitors whose first page of the day matched the row, which is how a referring site or campaign gets credit. Visitors who send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted at all, and neither are crawlers or link previews.
